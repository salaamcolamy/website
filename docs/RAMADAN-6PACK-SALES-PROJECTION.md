Based on market research and Ramadan/Raya purchase patterns in Malaysia (soda industry, past 2 years), plus TikTok engagement as a demand driver.

## 1. Market context (2023–2024)

| Source | Finding |
|--------|--------|
| **Ramadan spending** | 84% of Malaysians planned to increase spending during Ramadan; 94% willing to spend more on high-quality products for family and home. |
| **Retail growth** | Malaysia: +40% sales in first 2 weeks of Ramadan; **+103% peak** in first half of Ramadan (2023). YoY: +17% (2023 vs 2022), +21% transactions (2024 vs 2023). |
| **Soda category** | **+76% surge** in soda during Ramadan 2024. |
| **F&B category** | +23% during Ramadan 2023; complementary categories (e.g. cookies +66%, butter/margarine +49%) show strong family stocking for Iftar. |
| **Shopping behaviour** | Peak activity 6–7am (pre-dawn); browsing starts ~20 days before purchase. Effective selling window: **pre-Ramadan + Ramadan + Raya** (~6–8 weeks). |
| **Halal** | JAKIM halal is a key purchase driver for Muslim consumers; premium halal cola fits “quality for family” and gifting. |

**Implication:** Soda demand in Malaysia typically **at least doubles** in the Ramadan–Raya window vs baseline. Your 6-packs (family/berbuka/gifting) sit in the sweet spot.

## 3. Demand projection (Ramadan–Raya window)

### 3.1 Assumptions

- **Baseline:** Assume a “normal” month would sell X six-packs across all SKUs. For Ramadan–Raya we apply a **category multiplier** from the data above.
- **Ramadan multiplier:** Soda +76% (2024) and retail peaks ~2× in the first half of Ramadan → use a **1.8×–2.2×** multiplier for the combined Ramadan + Raya period (≈6–8 weeks) vs a typical month.
- **TikTok / viral factor:** Strong reactions and shares can add **+15–30%** on top of “normal” Ramadan demand for a small brand (awareness + trial intent). We use **+20%** in the base case.

### 3.2 Scenario definitions

## 6. How to get there (sell more)

1. **Pre-Ramadan (3–4 weeks before)**  
   - Launch 6-packs and mixed pack with a clear “Ramadan & Raya” message.  
   - Use TikTok/IG content (e.g. same style as the video that got strong reactions) to drive “where to buy” and “order for berbuka”.

2. **Distribution**  
   - Prioritise outlets that perform during Ramadan (bazaars, convenience near residential, mini marts, selected grocers).  
   - Push **mixed 6-pack** for Raya gifting and trial.

3. **Raya gifting**  
   - Position mixed and multi-6-pack as “Raya gift” or “balik kampung pack” to increase basket size.

4. **Bazaars / events**  
   - Even 1–2 bazaars with 6-packs (and single cans) can add 20–40% to local volume in that window.

5. **Track and adjust**  
   - Week 1–2 of Ramadan: if sell-through is strong, reorder quickly; the +76% soda trend often peaks in the first half of Ramadan.
