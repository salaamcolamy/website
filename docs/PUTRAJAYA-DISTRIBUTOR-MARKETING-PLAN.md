# Putrajaya Distributor – Marketing Plan  
**Salaam Cola Malaysia**

---

## 1. Executive summary

This plan supports the **Putrajaya distributor** to grow distribution and offtake of Salaam Cola in Putrajaya and nearby areas (e.g. Cyberjaya, Bangi) by focusing on **retail, F&B, corporate/B2B, and community/events**, aligned with the brand’s halal, premium and purpose-driven positioning.

---

## 2. Putrajaya market snapshot

| Factor | Notes |
|--------|--------|
| **Demographics** | Government staff, families, expats, students (nearby unis). Mix of Malay, Chinese, Indian; strong halal demand. |
| **Channels** | Convenience stores, petrol kiosks, mini marts, F&B (mamak, cafes, food courts, hotels), government canteens, corporate offices, events. |
| **Seasonality** | Ramadan & Raya, year-end, school terms, government events and open days. |
| **Competition** | Major colas and local halal/soft drinks in every channel. Differentiation = halal, taste, “Taste the Freedom” and purpose. |

---

## 3. Objectives (6–12 months)

1. **Distribution** – On-shelf in X targeted outlets (convenience, mini mart, F&B) in Putrajaya/Cyberjaya.
2. **Visibility** – Salaam Cola known as “the halal cola you can get in Putrajaya” (retail + F&B).
3. **Volume** – Steady offtake from key accounts (e.g. top 20 outlets + 5–10 corporate/event accounts).
4. **Brand** – Consistent messaging: premium halal cola, Taste the Freedom, purpose (e.g. 10% to causes).

---

## 4. Target segments & channels

| Segment | Channel examples | Priority |
|--------|-------------------|----------|
| **Retail** | Convenience (e.g. 7-Eleven, myNEWS, KK Mart), petrol mart, mini mart, selected grocery | High |
| **F&B** | Mamak, cafes, food courts, hotels, caterers | High |
| **Corporate / B2B** | Government offices, private offices, events, bulk/gifting | Medium |
| **Community / events** | Ramadan bazaars, community events, sports, open days | Medium |

---

## 5. Marketing tactics

### 5.1 Trade activation (distributor-led)

- **Outlet listing**  
  - List target outlets by area (e.g. Presint 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, Cyberjaya).  
  - Aim: secure placement in cold-drink chiller and/or end-cap.
- **Introductory offer**  
  - First order: small discount or free POS (poster/shelf strip) to secure trial.
- **Minimum order**  
  - Set a realistic minimum order per outlet to keep distribution efficient.
- **SOP**  
  - Visit frequency, order cycle, and basic merchandising standards (facing count, placement).

### 5.2 In-store & visibility

- **POS materials**  
  - Shelf wobblers, small posters, or counter cards with “Salaam Cola – Taste the Freedom” and halal badge.
- **Chiller placement**  
  - Prefer eye-level in chiller; if possible, block or mini-block for impact.
- **Price communication**  
  - Clear shelf/price tag so consumers see value vs. other colas.

### 5.3 Sampling & trial

- **Weekend sampling**  
  - High-footfall spots (e.g. IOI City Mall, Putrajaya Sentral, Alamanda, Taman Wetland) on selected weekends.
- **F&B trial**  
  - Leave cans/bottles with key F&B partners for staff and customer tasting before full listing.
- **Corporate sampling**  
  - Offer small sampling at government or corporate events (where allowed) to drive bulk/office orders.

### 5.4 Ramadan & Raya (peak period)

- **Ramadan bazaars**  
  - Presence (stall or partnership) at 1–2 Putrajaya/Cyberjaya bazaars; sell cans + promote “halal cola for berbuka”.
- **Raya gifting**  
  - Promote multipacks or gift boxes for Raya; target corporate and families.
- **Themed POS**  
  - Raya-themed poster or shelf strip for retail and F&B (if brand provides).

### 5.5 Digital & local awareness

- **Google Business Profile**  
  - Ensure distributor (or key pickup/warehouse) has a verified listing so “Salaam Cola Putrajaya” and “where to buy” searches surface.
- **Social (Facebook / Instagram)**  
  - Putrajaya/Cyberjaya targeted posts: “Now available in Putrajaya”, “Where to buy”, “Ramadan special”, F&B partner tags.
- **WhatsApp**  
  - Simple “Where to buy in Putrajaya” or “Bulk order Putrajaya” message for customers who contact the brand or distributor.

### 5.6 B2B & corporate

- **Office / event packs**  
  - Promote 24-can or 48-can packs for meetings, events, and Raya.
- **CSR angle**  
  - Use “10% to humanitarian causes” in B2B pitch for government and CSR-minded companies.
- **Contact**  
  - Use brand’s Join Us / Trade Enquiries (and contact page) to funnel Putrajaya B2B leads to this distributor.

---

## 6. Suggested timeline (12 months)

| Phase | Period | Focus |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Launch** | Month 1–2 | List target outlets, first orders, basic POS, 1–2 sampling days. |
| **Build** | Month 3–5 | Expand F&B and corporate; repeat sampling; ramp Google/social for Putrajaya. |
| **Ramadan / Raya** | Month 6–7 | Bazaar, Raya gifting, themed POS, peak stock and visibility. |
| **Consolidate** | Month 8–10 | Optimise top accounts, fix underperformers, gather feedback. |
| **Scale** | Month 11–12 | Add new outlets or nearby areas (e.g. Bangi); plan next year. |

---

## 7. KPIs to track

- **Distribution**  
  - Number of active outlets (by channel: retail vs F&B vs corporate).  
  - New listings per month.
- **Volume**  
  - Cases/cans per outlet per month; total Putrajaya offtake.
- **Visibility**  
  - Outlets with POS; chiller placement (eye-level vs other).
- **Brand**  
  - Google Business / “where to buy” visibility; social reach for Putrajaya posts.

---

## 8. Budget considerations (distributor)

- **POS** – Posters, shelf strips, wobblers (one-time or per wave).
- **Sampling** – Product, staff, and venue/booth if applicable.
- **Ramadan** – Bazaar stall fee, extra stock, Raya POS.
- **Digital** – Small boost for Putrajaya/Cyberjaya targeting (optional).
